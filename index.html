02 / RESEARCH STATEMENT

Research Statement

My research combines structural topology optimization, computational mechanics, and machine learning to develop efficient methods for structural design and scientific computation. I pursue three main directions: improving topology optimization algorithms, integrating artificial neural networks into optimization workflows, and developing AI-based equation solvers.

01 — STRUCTURAL TO

Structural topology optimization

I focus on improving algorithms for benchmark problems in structural topology optimization and translating new ideas into practical computational methods.

For stress-constrained weight-reduction design, I developed a constraint-shift strategy to address the discrepancy between relaxed and non-relaxed stresses, together with a body-fitted polygon mesh to improve boundary representation and stress evaluation. This work supports the development of binary structural designs under stress constraints [1].

I also developed a decoupled method for topology optimization under probabilistic constraints. By separating deterministic topology optimization from reliability analysis and coordinating them through constraint shifts, this method improves the efficiency of stress-constrained structural design under uncertainty [2].

02 — AI-ASSISTANT TO

AI-assistant TO

My aim is to use artificial neural networks (ANNs) to address practical challenges in topology optimization, particularly generalizability, efficiency, and accuracy.

In the efficient topology optimization (ETO) framework, structural updates are performed on a coarse grid, while a structure-mapping neural network directly maps density distributions to reconstruct the corresponding fine-grid design. Fragmentation and padding allow the trained network to be applied across design domains with different sizes and shapes [3].

I subsequently developed multi-grid, single-mesh online learning frameworks that use Fourier neural operators to predict sensitivity fields during optimization. The networks learn from data generated within the optimization process, without requiring a pre-collected training dataset. These frameworks address large-scale topology optimization and stress-constrained design based on an isogeometric formulation [4,5].

Another line of work uses radial basis function neural networks to represent structural density fields implicitly, reducing the number of design variables. A hybrid MMA–Adam scheme updates this representation to improve optimization efficiency [6].

My current research aims to develop effective design methods for stochastic mechanical metamaterials. I incorporate manufacturing-induced random variations into topology optimization and use HomoNet to accelerate homogenization. The corresponding manuscript is under review.

03 — AI EQUATION SOLVERS

Equation solvers based on AI

I study advanced neural operators and physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for efficiently solving systems of linear equations and parametric partial differential equations (PDEs).

I developed the decomposed Fourier neural operator (D-FNO), which learns mappings from parameter fields to solution fields. By decomposing high-dimensional latent representations into rank-one tensor products and using separable one-dimensional Fourier transforms, D-FNO reduces the computational cost of high-dimensional operator learning [7].

My ongoing work also investigates ANN acceleration of condensation methods for solving linear systems. The corresponding manuscript is under review.

04 — OTHER RESEARCH

Other research

My broader research includes graph-learning convolutional networks and hierarchical graph attention networks for semi-supervised node classification [8,9]. I have also used graph-based representations of assembly deviations and hierarchical graph attention to identify tolerances that should be prioritized for redesign using product usage data [10].

Future research directions

  • Over the next three to five years, I aim to develop the numerical methods and computational capabilities needed for topology optimization involving complex nonlinear behavior, coupled physical fields, and multidisciplinary design objectives.
  • Over a five-to-ten-year horizon, I plan to develop an integrated software platform for large-scale topology optimization, working toward billion-scale computational models that combine high-fidelity analysis with effective ANN acceleration.
